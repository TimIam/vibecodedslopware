How to play

Guess the five-letter word in six tries. Any real five-letter word counts as a guess.

Type or tap letters, then hit Enter. Each tile then shows how close you were:

Puzzles

Everyone starts with puzzle #1. After that you get the daily puzzle, the same for everyone and new at midnight your time. Random serves extra rounds.

Spooky time

Opt-in and off by default. Tick it (top right) and a startling image may jump onto the screen after some guesses. Silent, no strobing; untick to stop it at once.
