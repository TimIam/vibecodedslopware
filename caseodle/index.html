How to play

Guess the five-letter word in six tries. Any real five-letter word counts as a guess.

Type or tap letters, then hit Enter. Each tile then shows how close you were:

Grey: no K anywhere. A letter only lights up as many times as the answer uses it, so a repeat can go grey.

Puzzles

Everyone starts with puzzle #1. Finish it and a fresh puzzle is dealt every time: hit Next puzzle (or 🎲) for as many rounds as you like.

Spooky time

Opt-in and off by default. Tick it (top right) and a startling image may jump onto the screen after some guesses. Includes a sudden loud scream, with no strobing; untick to stop it at once.

Scream: Female Scream Horror by NeoPhyTe (public domain; shortened and faded). Zombie image from Anthony / TioMothy’s corn maze game. A spinning black-cat celebration with looping audio plays after a solve in either mode, ending with one meow. Close or Next puzzle stops it; use your device or tab volume. Reduced motion keeps the cat still.
