QUARTERLY ROLLOUT
An office-chair action game from Ambivalent Software (a made-up publisher; probably fun, we are not entirely sure).

Made for vibecodedslopware.com by TimIam with an AI pair programmer, 2026.

Everything in this folder was written new for this game: the code, the six floor layouts, the story and
dialogue, the characters (Bea Swivelton, Old Squeaky, Velma Bottomline, Dale from Facilities, Calendar Carl,
BUFF-E), the canvas-drawn art, the synthesised sound effects and the music ("Rollout Shuffle" and
"Lobby Hold Music"). There are no image, font or audio files: art is drawn with canvas paths, text uses the
fonts already on your device, and sound is generated live with the Web Audio API.

Paperweight & Partners, its staff and its printers are fictional.
