Windows Across the Street

An eight-story brick walk-up across a wet city street at night, inspired by After Dark: Voyeur. Twenty-four connected multi-window apartments and forty-one autonomous residents living quiet, unscripted lives—until a delivery van, fire truck, or detective squad rolls up outside.

The Windows .scr is unsigned, so your browser may warn “not commonly downloaded” and Windows SmartScreen may ask to confirm. Click Keep, then More info → Run anyway. It is a tiny C# WebView2 host built with Windows’ built-in .NET compiler; the full source is in windows-across/src.

Install as a Windows screensaver

  1. Download WindowsAcrossTheStreet.scr and save it somewhere permanent (such as your Documents folder).
  2. Right-click the file and choose Install. Windows Screen Saver Settings opens with Windows Across the Street selected.
  3. Click Settings… inside Screen Saver Settings at any time to choose your default season, weather, or anti-burn-in mode—your choices save automatically for screensaver runs.
  4. When idle, it fills every monitor with no cursor or UI; move the mouse or press any key to return.

Mac, Linux & kiosk mode

  • Browser fullscreen: open the live web app and press F for fullscreen or H to hide the HUD.
  • macOS screensaver: with WebViewScreenSaver, point it at https://vibecodedslopware.com/windows-across/windows.html#embed for pure frameless playback.

What happens behind the glass

  • True multi-room apartments: Every unit spans three connected windows (kitchen, living room, bedroom, office, sewing room, studio, or kids’ room). Residents walk behind the brick piers from room to room, switching lights, TVs, and monitors as they go.
  • 117 procedural behaviors & street responses: Most of the building stays calm and unhurried—cooking, sewing, watching TV, sleeping—so rare events genuinely catch your eye: Amazon-style parcel deliveries with unboxing, kitchen fires with a red fire truck and firefighters, crime cover-ups followed by a police cruiser, yellow tape, and forensic detectives, medical calls with an ambulance, burglaries, and UFO abductions.
  • Seasons, holidays & weather: Winter features snow-dusted ledges, twinkling Christmas trees, and three Jewish households lighting glowing Hanukkah menorahs (with no Christmas trees); Autumn brings jack-o’-lanterns, window silhouettes, and drifting leaves; Spring brings flower boxes and rain; Summer brings warm clear skies, window AC units, and fireflies.
  • Spaced anti-burn-in protection: Instead of drifting constantly, the camera rests rock-steady and performs a whisper-slow, almost imperceptible micro-shift every ~45–75 seconds to protect OLED and CRT displays.
