# Assets (`library.glb`, `stage_1..5.glb`)

Everything in this folder except `preview.html` is generated by `build_assets.py` (DESIGN.md §15-§16). Do not hand-edit the GLBs.

**Rebuild** (Blender 5.1.2, ~30 s; the script re-reads every GLB afterwards and exits 1 on any failed check):

```
"C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 5.1\blender.exe" --background --factory-startup --python datacenter/build_assets.py
```

Set `ASSET_REPORT=<file.json>` in the environment to also get a per-object summary (dimensions, tris, children). View the result at
`datacenter/assets/preview.html?file=library.glb` (`&focus=a,b`, `&bright=1`, `&axes=1`, `&view=front|iso|top`, `&stage=1` keeps a shell's own layout).

## Conventions

- 1 unit = 1 m, three.js Y up. Blender's **−Y is the front** and `export_yup` turns it into **+Z in three.js**: every unit faces +Z, LEDs and
  louvres are on the +Z face, a rack's open front is +Z. Racks in a row that faces the camera (data.js `rotY 0`) need no rotation.
- Every library object has its **origin at the bottom-centre** (min y = 0, x/z centred) and its transforms applied, except the shared-datablock
  variants, which keep a **node scale** (table below). Flat shading, no textures, no lights, no cameras, no animations.
- ≤ 400 tris per library mesh (largest: `pwr_hv_feed` 386 incl. its pylon child); shells are 790-1,708 tris each.
- Object names are the ids in `data.js`. **Repeated child names** (`led_0`, `led_1`, `fan_0`, `slot_0..7`) are exact in the GLB, but three.js's
  `GLTFLoader` de-duplicates node names across a file (`led_0`, `led_0_2`, `led_0_3`, ...). Every such child therefore also carries
  `userData.role` (`"led_0"`, `"fan_0"`, `"slot_3"`, ...): match on `child.userData.role`, or rely on child order (led_0 is always before led_1,
  slots are in order). Variants carry `userData.base` (the object whose datablock they share).
- Shared datablocks (same glTF mesh index, so `InstancedMesh` can reuse one geometry): `srv_pizza_box` = `srv_blade`; `srv_ai_pod` = `srv_sled`;
  `cool_crac` = `cool_chiller` (×2.0) = `cool_cryo` (×3.0); `pwr_substation` = `pwr_hv_feed` (×1.6); `net_fiber_uplink` = `net_core_router` (×1.5)
  = `net_ix_port` (×2.0) = `net_cable_landing` (×2.5). Each variant object has its own `led_*` empties / detail children; children of a scaled
  variant are authored in its local space and scale with it.

- `rack_frame.slot_i` = `(0, 0.12 + 0.22·i, 0.10)`: put a server's origin here; its front then sits 0.05 m behind the rack's front posts.
  The rack's own origin is its floor centre.

- `fan_0` (mesh child of `cool_box_fan`, `cool_crac`, `cool_tower`): **spins about its own local +Y** — call `fan.rotateY(speed·dt)`, never set

## Decisions and deviations from the spec

- Rack "two materials" = material slot 0 paint (`MAT_steel_dark`) + slot 1 fixed dark trim (`MAT_plastic`); the Colo repaint replaces slot 0
  with `MAT_rack_white` (present in `library.glb` on the CRAC body, AC units, etc.). Servers are not repainted.
- Server bodies are 0.17-0.20 m tall (not 1U) so a full rack reads as a solid stack at 0.22 m pitch; all fit the 0.52 × 0.9 × 0.20 envelope.
- `cool_cryo` cannot have a different material than `cool_crac` while sharing the datablock (glTF materials live on the mesh), so its tint is a
  `MAT_coolant` detail child plus `userData.tint`.
- Added `MAT_glow` (strength 0.7) for large architectural fixtures and `MAT_tile_hot`/`MAT_tile_blue` for the tinted floor strips; the extra prop
  colours are `MAT_yellow`, `MAT_gold`, `MAT_red`, `MAT_wood`, `MAT_rubber`, `MAT_cardboard`; `MAT_glow_blue` / `MAT_glow_red` are tinted glows for
  the Colo vending front and (blue) the Hall aisle strips; both have their own shading class in the scene.
- `prop_satellite` and the `tower_0..2` empties are extra top-level objects in `stage_5.glb` (a blinking satellite has to be its own object).
- Fans spin about their local +Y; the box fan's `fan_0` is the only child node with a rotation.
- Chain-link fences in the Warehouse yard and around the substation use `MAT_chainlink` and so depend on the runtime texture, like the Colo cage.
- The fiber cabinet is 0.4 m wide (data.js asks ≤ 0.42 for the Hall lane); at ×2.5 `net_cable_landing` is 1.0 m and relies on the scene's
  uniform scale-down for the 1.0 m Campus lane pitch (fit 0.9). `cool_cryo` at ×3.0 is 2.46 m wide against the Hall yard's 2.4 m fit (same rule).
