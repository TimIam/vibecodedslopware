Controls

W A S D
Advance, strafe, or back away. You face your rival automatically.
Drag left mouse
Steer your weapon. Reverse the drag to swing back.
Right drag / K
Right drag braces a single weapon or swings your left weapon when holding two. K braces either loadout. Angle the blade into contact to block.
Space
Hold to gather, release to jump. Longer holds give a higher hop.
Shift
Hold to lower your stance and run. Forward steps cover more ground than backsteps.
Ctrl
Hold to crouch and walk low.
R / F
Hold R for a short grip. Tap F to thrust.
Q / E
Tap Q for the left hand or E for the right. A free hand grabs a nearby rival or picks up a weapon. Tap again to release. Crouch to favor a weapon on the ground.
X
Exchange the weapons in your hands.
J / arrow keys
Swing with J or steer the blade with the arrows.
Touch
Move with the left pad. Drag the arena to steer, or tap Swing.
