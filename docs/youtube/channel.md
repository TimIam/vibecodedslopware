Both images are built from a frozen frame of /splash.js at 3.4s (`?splash=3.4`, 320×180): the background, the
logo and the blob were rendered as separate layers, then scaled up by whole numbers so the pixels stay crisp
(background and blob 8×, logo 4× so it fits the safe area).
