Pour Decisions

You just got hired behind the bar at The Tipsy Comet, a dive on Ludlow Street. The regulars are a lot: a chess hustler, a tugboat captain, an 82-year-old martini fiend, and Orbital, a bear who has been to space.

How to tend

  • Card everyone. Tap Check ID and compare: does the photo look like them? Does the first name match what they told you? Are they 21 by tonight's date? Is the card expired? Real cards have a shiny hologram and no typos. Serve a minor and you're fired on the spot.
  • Mix it right. Pick a glass, add ice if it's served on the rocks, then press and hold a bottle to free-pour (about 1 oz a second; a quick tap is ¼ oz). Choose Build, Stir, Shake or Blend, add the garnish, and Serve.
  • The recipe book is always there, but peeking while a customer waits halves your tip, and the clock keeps ticking.
  • Keep the jukebox going. Silence sours the room. Everybody has a favorite genre, and they'll hint at it.
  • Charm them. Chat once per drink. Some people want you to listen, some want a laugh, some want a compliment.
  • Watch the drunks. Rosy cheeks are fine. Swaying, droopy eyes and hiccups mean Cut Off: serve water, no more booze. Over-serving is a strike. Three strikes and you're out.
