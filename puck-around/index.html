    How to play

    WASD or the arrow keys skate. A controller works too.

    With the puck: J passes toward where you are steering, and you take over the skater you passed to. K shoots at the marker sliding across the goal mouth: tap for a quick wrister, hold and let go for a slapshot. Shoot when the marker is away from the goalie.

    Without it: J switches you to the skater nearest the puck. K throws a body check.

    Faceoffs: hit J the moment the puck drops. Too early and your stick is tied up.

    Fights: check the same skater enough and the gloves come off. AD move, hold W to guard your head or S your body, J jabs high, K digs low. The loser sits five minutes and their team skates short.

    Z and X work in place of J and K. Esc opens the menu.
