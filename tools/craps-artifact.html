    Pick a chip, then click a bet on the felt or below. Right-click or shift-click takes one off. Bets stay up until they lose or you take them down. A pass line bet is locked in once a point is set.

    Place bets are off on the come-out roll

    Hardways are off on the come-out roll. They lose on a 7 or when the number rolls the easy way.

    Set the dice

    Set dice come to your hand already arranged. A smooth, straight flick with no shaking throws them with backspin on one axis, so the set really changes what comes up. Shake hard or throw crooked and they tumble.

    Throw

    Grab the dice on the felt, shake if you like, then flick toward the far wall and let go. The space bar throws for you.
