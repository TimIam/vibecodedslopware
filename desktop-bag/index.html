Desktop Bag

A plastic bag drifts across your desktop while you work. Your mouse is the wind.

There’s one on this page. Move your mouse near it.

Download for Windowsv0.1.0 · 71 MB · free · nothing to install Try it in your browseron a fake desktop

It’s unsigned, so Windows will probably say “Windows protected your PC.” Click More info, then Run anyway. The first launch takes about ten seconds while it unpacks.

How it plays

  • The bag floats in a see-through layer over everything. Clicks go straight through to your apps, and the taskbar is the ground.
  • While it’s in the air it earns beauty. The longer it stays up, the more grace builds.
  • Moments pay extra: a loop, suspended (hanging almost perfectly still), it almost landed, you lifted it, dancing with a leaf.
  • Hover the little counter in the bottom-right corner to spend beauty on more wind, warm pavement, eddies, company, and better cameras. It goes phone → camcorder → 16mm → 35mm → your own eyes.
  • End the tape to put it on the shelf and start a new season: October, first snow, April, midsummer fireflies.
