Boss Hours2The Sequel

The first sequel ever released before its predecessor was finished.

  • You are the boss. Again. Walk the path between the rocks; touching each rock in turn builds Aimlessness, which multiplies everything.
  • Points are XP now. Level up and pick one of three cards: new abilities, upgrades, health, faster legs. Common, Good, Great, Legendary. Every pick shows up on the boss.
  • 5-mans wander in; 10 laps brings a 25-man raid, 20 the 40-man, which ends the map. Raid wipes pay a bonus card. The quiet man on a horse is back: shake him off.
