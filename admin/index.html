How to read this
  • Numbers in the window are totals over the chosen dates. Lifetime numbers (itch.io, CurseForge, GitHub) are the store's running total on the date shown, not limited to the window, because those stores publish no history.
  • Store clicks count visitors who clicked through to a store. A click is not a purchase or a download.
  • unknown means no data yet for something the store can report. n/a means the store has no official source for it. Neither is zero.
  • partial means the window reaches past what the source has covered; hover for details.
  • Revenue is shown per currency and never converted or added across currencies.
