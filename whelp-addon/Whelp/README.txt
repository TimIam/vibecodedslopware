Whelp v1.0.0 - real reviews from real party members.

Rate your tanks, healers and DPS out of five stars, tick highlights
("Steady pulls", "Stood in fire", "Needed on everything"...), and leave
a 140-character review. Ratings show on player tooltips, and you get a
heads-up in chat when a reviewed player joins your group.

  /whelp              write a review of your party, or a recent one
  /whelp Name-Realm   read a player's reviews (their "business page")
  /whelp target       read your target's reviews
  /whelp me           read reviews other Whelp users wrote about you
  /whelp prompt       ask to review after a dungeon (on)
  /whelp share        share your reviews with other Whelp users (on)
  /whelp tooltip      ratings on tooltips (on)
  /whelp alerts       chat alert when a rated player joins (on)

Right-click a player for "Write a review" and "See reviews".

How sharing works: reviews travel over guild and group addon chat, and
each player only ever sends their own reviews, so nobody can post under
someone else's name. Reviewers with fewer than 3 reviews are filed under
"not recommended". Three or more recommended reviews averaging under 2
stars earn a Consumer Alert. Reviewers with 20+ reviews are Elite.

Your last 15 parties are saved, so you can review people after the group
breaks up. A run ends when you leave the group, enter another instance,
or stay outside the instance for two minutes while alive (corpse runs
don't count). You're asked for reviews if the run lasted 4+ minutes in
an instance or killed a boss.
