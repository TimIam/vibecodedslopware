Party Health Pie

Your whole 5-man party in one circle. A radial health, resource, pet, shield and incoming-heal meter for World of Warcraft: Classic Forever and retail Midnight.

An early release. Bars are a shape, and the shape can be a pie.

Download for Classic Foreverv1.1.0 · 0.3 MB · free Download for retail (Midnight)v1.1.0 · 0.3 MB · free · adds all 13 classes

The retail build hasn’t been played on a live retail client yet, so treat it as a test release. The debuff glow is new in 1.1.0 and hasn’t been tested in a real group yet.

Install

  1. Exit World of Warcraft.
  2. Unzip the download. You get a folder called PartyHealthPie.
  3. Move that folder into the AddOns folder for your version of the game: Classic Forever: C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns Retail: C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns On a Mac, the folder starts with /Applications/World of Warcraft/. If you installed the game somewhere else, look there.
  4. Check the folder isn’t doubled. This file should exist: ...\AddOns\PartyHealthPie\PartyHealthPie.toc
  5. Start the game. On the character select screen, click AddOns and make sure Party Health Pie is ticked.
  6. Try it. Type /php demo in chat to see a sample party, and the same again to stop.

If it doesn’t show up

  • Not in the AddOns list? Look for a doubled folder like AddOns\PartyHealthPie\PartyHealthPie\, and make sure the download matches the folder: the Classic Forever zip goes in _forever_, the retail zip in _retail_.
  • Marked out of date? Tick Load out of date AddOns on the same screen.
  • Updating? Exit the game, delete the old folder and install the new one. Your settings are kept.

How to read it

  • Health fills the outer 3/5 of a wedge in class color and sinks toward the center as it drops.
  • Resources fill the inner 2/5 and drain outward toward the health line.
  • Pets take a full-height slice, 33% of their owner’s wedge.
  • Shields are a blue band from the rim inward, sized by % of max health.
  • Incoming heals are a pale green band starting where health ends.
  • Low health (33% or below) turns the wedge deep red with a thick flashing outline.
  • Dead is charcoal, disconnected is grey, and someone who left leaves a transparent wedge.
  • Your target gets a yellow outline. Select a pet and only its slice lights up.
  • Role symbols mark each player as tank (shield), healer (cross) or DPS (sword).
  • Debuffs you can dispel turn the symbol curse purple, magic blue, disease gold or poison green. A halo fades as the debuff runs out, and a countdown shows the seconds left.

Healing with it

  • Hover a wedge and press a heal key to cast on that player or pet. Hover a pet’s slice to heal the pet.
  • Left-click a wedge to target them.
  • On first login the addon turns on the game’s Mouseover Cast option so hovering works, and tells you in chat. You can change it under Options > Combat, or turn off Mouseover Healing in the addon’s settings.

Make it yours

  • Drag it anywhere, and resize it from 90 to 420 pixels.
  • Recolor every class, resource, class pet, low-health, shield, incoming-heal and target color.
  • Pick a border: Steel, Iron, Silver, Gold or Brass.
  • Set border and player color opacity separately.
  • Turn pet wedges and resources on or off for each class.
  • On retail, tick Secondary for a class to show its combo points, holy power, soul shards, chi, arcane charges, essence or runes in place of its main power.

Good to know

  • WoW locks mouseover targets during combat. If the party changes mid-fight, wedges may shuffle by party position until combat ends, but each wedge always shows who a hover would hit.
  • The circle can’t be dragged during combat, and size changes apply when combat ends.
  • A low-health wedge stays red until the player recovers to 50%. When the game hides party health from addons, it follows the 33% line both ways instead.
  • Instant heals have no cast, so they don’t show an incoming-heal band.
  • Role symbols come from the role set in the game, so someone with no role has no symbol. They still get the debuff halo and countdown.
  • If a player has several debuffs you can dispel, the symbol shows the one ending soonest.
  • On retail, the game may not report other players’ secondary resources. If a class with Secondary ticked shows an empty band, untick it.
