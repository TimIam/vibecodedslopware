Wasteland Planning CommissionA post-apocalyptic city builder

The world ended. The paperwork did not. You are the Planning Commission of a dusty outpost with one farm, five settlers and a lot of junk.

  • Clear debris to salvage scrap, wood, stone, metal and glass. Build homes and farms; surplus food draws settlers.
  • Recruit security before the mutants and raiders find you. Your people are tiny and very breakable.
  • Research from scrap shacks to timber, then brick and glass, then plastic and its colourful vinyl siding.
  • Fulfil the Charter: parks, shops, a newspaper, factory and battery farms, wind and solar, electric cars, and a Mobile Settlement Vehicle to found the next town.
