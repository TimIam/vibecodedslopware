# How the Half Sword demo played

The early Steam Abyss gives this project its encounter loop. The later arena playtest gives it a reference for weapon handling and short duels. Half Sword Games developed the two builds separately and combined them in April 2025. The studio subsequently changed combat and locomotion again for Early Access. [First playtest announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2397300/announcements/detail/6189717974112396244) · [Combined demo announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2397300/announcements/detail/536598373660098851) · [Developer retrospective](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2397300/announcements/detail/512986184073347345)

## What changed between builds

| Build | What a player could do |
| --- | --- |
| Early itch.io prototype | Move the mouse to move the sword in a physically simulated fight. The creator labels the February 2023 download *Gauntlet* 0.2.4.2. The name predates the ranked mode added in 2025. [Creator's prototype page](https://knives-frank.itch.io/halfsword) |
| Steam Abyss, October 2023 | Players of the October 29 build describe scavenging equipment, surviving ordinary encounters, facing a scythe-bearing boss and then finding new equipment. Accounts differ on how early the boss appeared. A later first-person account recalls the dark exploration space. These are observations of particular runs. [October 29 encounter reports](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2397300/discussions/0/3874844466808847689/) · [Contemporary gear and boss reports](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2397300/discussions/0/3874844033665106647/) · [Returning player's description](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2397300/discussions/0/509578026046989560/) |
| Arena playtest, July–August 2024 | A closed map tested controls, armor and weapon weight. An August update adjusted long and heavy weapons, enabled hand switching for two-handed weapons, and offered cards for single or group combat. Factions wore distinguishable clothing and shields. [July announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2397300/announcements/detail/6189717974112396244) · [August playtest update](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2397300/announcements/detail/4335362463053532492) |
| Arena playtest, November 2024 | The developer added a practice dummy and kicking, revised parrying and grip changes, and strengthened armor against blunt damage. The same update added several levels of visual impact feedback, including clothing damage. [November playtest notes](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2397300/announcements/detail/4442333105001333412) |
| Combined demo, April–May 2025 | Gauntlet victories earned points toward ranks. A Gauntlet death sent the character to the Abyss, where a further death erased the run. Free Mode gave immediate access to all but the last rank. The May update reduced falls, shortened recovery and adjusted weapon-versus-armor balance. [April demo notes](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2397300/announcements/detail/536598373660098851) · [May demo notes](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2397300/announcements/detail/536602812925083876) |

## Design implications for a browser duel

These are implementation targets inferred from those builds. They also give a playtester specific differences to look for.

| Mechanic | What to look for in a playable build |
| --- | --- |
| Direct control | Reversing the mouse mid-motion changes the path of the on-screen weapon. The fighter's arms follow the weapon, and horizontal and vertical input produce visibly different poses. [Creator's prototype](https://knives-frank.itch.io/halfsword) · [Studio description](https://halfswordgames.com/games/halfsword/) |
| Weight and reach | Under the same input, a heavier weapon takes longer to reverse. Its longer silhouette and contact reach agree. These differences should be legible before either fighter loses health. [August playtest update](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2397300/announcements/detail/4335362463053532492) |
| Contact and defense | A miss passes through empty space. A blade interception produces recoil and a distinguishable sound. Sustained contact should resolve as one exchange until the players separate or begin a new motion. [Studio gameplay overview](https://halfswordgames.com/games/halfsword/) · [November playtest notes](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2397300/announcements/detail/4442333105001333412) |
| Balance and recovery | Movement and contact can upset a fighter's pose. A recoverable stumble and a round-ending fall need distinct feedback. The May demo patch is a useful tuning reference if players spend too much time waiting to regain control. [May demo notes](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2397300/announcements/detail/536602812925083876) |
| Scavenging and stakes | In an Abyss-inspired practice run, a fresh item or stronger opponent gives the next encounter a reason to exist. Equipment positioned in the arena lets movement affect which item a player can claim. [October player observations](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2397300/discussions/0/3874844466808847689/) |
| Online interpretation | A two-player room adapts the playtest's duel option. Both clients should see the same clash, victor and rematch state. Distinct tabard colors keep the fighters identifiable together. Online rooms and their scoring are design decisions for this project. [Playtest duel and faction design](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2397300/announcements/detail/4335362463053532492) |
