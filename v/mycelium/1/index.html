MYCELIUM

a colony sim in the SimAnt tradition, for fungi

You are a fungus. Grow a network through the soil, find things to eat, out-fight the rival colony and the grubs, then push up mushrooms and shed spores to drift onto the next plot. Take the whole garden.

How to play

Each plot is a slice of soil seen from the side. Your colony starts at the pulsing white square. The rival starts at the orange one.

Grow

  • Bright dots on the ends of your threads are tips. They wander, branch, and leave hyphae behind. Growing costs energy.
  • Click or drag in the soil to lay attractant. Tips steer toward it. Draw a trail to the orange-brown food: dead roots, leaf litter, buried logs.
  • Hyphae on food pull energy back along the network. Cut-off threads turn grey and wither, so keep the network whole.
  • Dry soil (pale) slows growth and kills threads. Rain soaks the topsoil; water pockets keep the deep soil wet. Rock is impassable; clay is slow.

Allocate

  • The triangle splits your effort. Grow gives more tips. Defend makes toxins (a purple stain) that hurt the rival and grubs on contact. Fruit pushes mushrooms up where your network touches the surface.

Fruit and spread

  • Grown mushrooms shed spores for a while, then wilt. Spores are your breeders: they drift to the rest of the garden.
  • Shed the target number of spores on a plot, or wipe out the rival, to claim it. On the garden map, spend banked spores to drift onto a plot next to one you hold. Spores count even if you lose the plot.

Threats

  • The rival grows toward the same food and eats into your threads where you meet. Density and toxins decide fights.
  • Each colony has a core (the pulsing square) with five hearts. Reach the rival's with dense, toxic hyphae to consume it and win the plot outright. Guard your own.
  • Grubs chew through anything. Toxins kill them, and a dead grub is food.
  • The gardener occasionally digs. Nothing survives a spade.
