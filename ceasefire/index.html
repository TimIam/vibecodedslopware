    UNITED NATIONS · DEPARTMENT OF PEACE OPERATIONS

    CEASEFIRE

    Two generals are playing chess. Your job is to make sure nobody wins.

    The generals play real chess against each other. Each one wants checkmate.

    You deploy duck-egg-blue peacekeepers. After a move you can walk a new one onto the a- or h-file (the sidelines), drop one next to a helmet already on the board, step a helmet one square, or abstain.

    Peacekeepers can't be captured and block everything except knights, which jump over them.

    You win on a stalemate: the side to move has no legal moves and is not in check. You lose on a checkmate, or when your mandate runs out.

      Plain chess, minus castling and en passant. Pawns promote to queens.
