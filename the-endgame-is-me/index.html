The Endgame is Me: a reverse boss fight

Needs a keyboard and mouse.

Handbook

You are the boss. The job is to walk from a rock to another rock, forever. Adventurers keep interrupting.

  • Walk the path between the rocks for points. Touch each rock in turn to build Aimlessness, which multiplies everything.
  • Step off the path and Aimlessness fades. Go past your leash and you evade: you heal, but the multiplier resets.
  • 5-mans wander in (one is always AFK). After 10 laps a 25-man raid shows up; they count down, then dodge your telegraphs because they read the guide. At 20 laps comes the 40-man, and nothing else.
  • A quiet man on a horse will try to climb your fur and stab the glowing spot on your back. Shake him off, then step on him.
  • Beat the 40-man and the Hoard opens: spend your points on new abilities (adds, a lightning beam, ice, a stun charge, zoomies) and upgrades. Then the next level. Horns, tails, wings and fur colour are free in Settings.
  • After the Clearing the job changes: woods, farms, villages, a city wall and a castle, all there to be flattened. Wrecking things builds Vengeance. Flatten the castle and you can go home.
